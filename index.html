About Us

Adron Homes Properties is one of Nigeria’s leading real estate and property development companies, renowned for delivering secure, affordable and premium housing solutions tailored to individuals and families across all economic classes.

Driven by innovation and excellence, the company has continued to transform the real estate landscape through world-class developments, strategic infrastructure investments and customer-centric solutions that create sustainable long-term value. Beyond developing properties by our arrays of professionals, Adron Homes is committed to building thriving communities where dreams are nurtured, investments are protected and futures are secured. With a rapidly expanding presence across Nigeria and a vision aligned with global real estate standards, the company continues to position itself as a proudly African brand with international relevance and impact.

Through visionary leadership, integrity, innovation and an unwavering passion for development, Adron Homes is redefining excellence while creating communities that compete with the best across the world.
